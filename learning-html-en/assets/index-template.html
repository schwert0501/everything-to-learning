💬 Learning Tips

How to get the most out of this material
  • Study the chapters in order. Start each chapter with the "Learning Objectives", check them off as you go, and your progress is saved automatically in the browser (it is lost if you switch browsers or clear the cache).
  • For each swimlane/architecture diagram, first re-explain it to yourself in your own words, then read the note below it — you only truly understand it when you can explain it out loud.
  • For the self-check questions, don't just read the answers — write your own answer from memory first.
  • Can't see a diagram? It may be because you opened the page offline: diagrams automatically degrade to their source text. Refresh while online to render them again.
